# Campus Hostel Rules (Mock Document)

1. Students must carry their college identity card while entering the hostel.
2. The standard in-time is 9:00 PM from Monday to Saturday.
3. Visitors must register at the hostel reception.
4. Students must keep rooms and common areas clean.
5. Loud music and disturbances are not allowed after 10:00 PM.
6. Any maintenance issue should be reported to the hostel warden.
7. Cooking with personal heating appliances is not allowed in rooms.
8. These are fictional rules created only for this assignment. They are not official rules of any real university.
